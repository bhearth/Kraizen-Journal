RADIO KOTAGIRI

A community radio station in the Nilgiris, established in 2014 as the first in the district. For 12 years, it has amplified local voices, addressed grassroots issues, and given tribal youth a platform to be heard.

KEYSTONE FOUNDATION

Founded in Kotagiri in 1993, working with indigenous communities and traditional honey-hunters. Now operates across the Nilgiris Biosphere and beyond — in Pillur, Sigur, Wayanad, Sathyamangalam, Nilambur, and Potka (Jharkhand).

1993 Nilgiris Multi-State
🌳 🏛️

AUROVILLE

Founded in 1968 as a universal township of human unity. Now exploring ties with states across India — from Gujarat to a possible presence at the 2036 Olympics. A model for sustainable architecture, waste management, and integral education.

1968 Pondicherry Global Reach

🌍 WHAT AUROVILLE HAS ACHIEVED — AND WHAT OTHERS ARE LEARNING

🤝

STATE COLLABORATIONS

Auroville has signed seven MoUs with universities in Gujarat for sustainability, architecture, waste management, and probiotics. It is now exploring similar ties with other states [citation:4].

🏫

INTEGRAL EDUCATION

The philosophy of Integral Education — where freedom given to the child leads to discovering their true nature — has inspired schools across India. Auroville's educational model is being shared with over 300 rural schools nationwide [citation:13].

🌐

GLOBAL REACH

The "integral-yoga-talks" website makes Sri Aurobindo and The Mother's teachings freely available worldwide [citation:11]. Auroville's model is being studied and adapted in multiple countries.

🌾

RURAL INITIATIVES

The AID school, serving children from surrounding villages, was among the first community welfare initiatives. A new university is being planned for rural youth and start-up initiatives [citation:2][citation:4].

🪨 THE PROPOSAL — LAYING SIMPLE STONES, ONE BY ONE

The vision is not to replicate Auroville. It is to evolve beyond it — to make people live outside homes and offices, more natural, more connected. Every village, every place, every city must step up from the learnings of the past.

🚪
MOVE OUT

Out of the office. Out of the AC room. Into the sun, the rain, the soil.

🌿
BECOME NATURAL

Live with the rhythms of nature — not against them. Food, water, air, light.

🪨
LAY STONES

Simple stones, one by one. Not a grand plan. Just the next right step.

🧱
BUILD SKILLS

With natural and organic substances. Building new skills in people, not just structures.

EARTH ENGINEERING — 40 MILLION INSPIRATORS

There may be around 40 million people on Earth who are ready to inspire. People who have walked through their own patterns and found something real. People who can light a fire in others — not by teaching, but by being.

This number should grow. Not through marketing. Through lived example. Through one person seeing another and thinking: "If they can, I can."
